# Fighter Craft

A 3D robot-fighting adventure for young heroes. Nova City was the richest city in the world
until the robot boss CORE-X stole its power. Run freely through the city streets, beat the five
bosses, win back the five pieces of legendary armor, and watch the city come back to life.

- **Open 3D city, third-person view.** A normal human hero with smooth, rounded graphics.
  Walk anywhere between the buildings, climb any wall (run into it and hold JUMP) and find
  treasure on the rooftops.
- **Vehicles.** Press F to call the hero car and drive it (you can ram robots). The airport
  sells a helicopter and a fighter jet. Press F in the air to jump out - a parachute opens.
- **Robot bases.** Each robot-held district has a base with turrets that sends out patrol
  robots. Blow it up (the jet and helicopter are best) and the district boss gets 25% weaker.
- **Robo-Pals - the heart of the game.** The robots are not evil; CORE-X hacked them. Beaten
  robots drop cores, and at the Robo Lab cores rebuild a robot as your own teammate that
  follows you and fights at your side. Five kinds to craft and level up.
- **Walk-in buildings.** Step on a glowing ring to use the Dojo, Workshop, Bank, Hospital,
  Power Plant, Hero Park, Armor Hall, Robo Lab, airport office or the Rumble stadium.
- **Five districts.** Each has a glowing beacon where its missions start. Patrol robots roam
  the streets of districts that are not saved yet.
- **Weapons.** Power Sword, Thunder Hammer, Tri-Shot and Rocket Launcher are bought with coins
  at the Workshop. Armor is never bought - it is won from bosses.

## How to play

- **On this PC:** double-click `Play Fighter Craft.bat` (or just open `index.html` in any browser).
- **From a link:** host the folder on GitHub Pages (see below) and open the link on any PC, tablet or phone.

No install, no downloads, no internet needed once the files are on the PC. The whole game is
under 400 KB - all graphics, sounds and music are generated by code.

| Action | Keyboard | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| Run forward / back up | ↑ ↓ or W S | stick / d-pad |
| Turn | ← → or A D | stick / d-pad |
| Jump; hold against a wall to climb it | Space | A |
| Car: call it, get in / out of any vehicle | F | Y |
| Helicopter down | Shift | LT |
| Hit with fists or weapon (press 3x for a combo finisher) | J or X, or mouse click | X |
| Blaster | K or C | B |
| Super (when the gold bar is full) | L or V | RT |
| Swap close-up weapon / blaster | Q / E | LB / RB |
| Pause, power, badges, settings | Esc | Start |

Tablets and phones get an on-screen thumb-stick and buttons automatically.
The game needs WebGL (any current Edge, Chrome, Firefox or Safari).

## What it teaches

- **Power is earned, not given.** The Power Level is a visible sum of level, training, armor
  and city. Tap the ⚡ POWER chip in the city to see exactly where every point comes from.
- **Read the numbers.** Every robot shows its Power Level (green = ready, red = train first).
- **Practice pays off.** Three Dojo trainings: number puzzles (Strength), reaction + arrow keys
  (Speed) and pattern memory (Focus). Math questions adapt - they get harder as he gets better.
- **Saving works.** The Bank pays interest on coins that are *kept*, after every mission.
- **Armor is never bought.** It is a free reward for beating a boss - and each piece upgrades the
  whole city, which unlocks bigger buildings.
- **Spend wisely.** Coins buy buildings and weapons, so there is always a choice between
  spending now and saving for something better.
- **Losing is fine.** A lost fight keeps the XP and half the coins, and BOLT explains what to
  train next. BOLT reads tips and questions out loud (can be switched off in ⚙ settings).

## Progress

Progress is saved automatically in the browser on that device. "Start over" is in ⚙ settings.

## Hosting on GitHub Pages

1. Push this folder to a **public** GitHub repository.
2. Repository → Settings → Pages → Source: *Deploy from a branch* → `main` / `(root)` → Save.
3. After a minute the game is live at `https://<user>.github.io/<repo>/`.

## For developers

Vanilla JavaScript, no build step and no dependencies. The 3D world is drawn by a small
hand-written WebGL renderer (boxes plus smooth spheres, cylinders and cones); the HUD, title and Dojo use a 2D canvas on top.

- `js/core.js` - input, synthesized sound/music, save, power math
- `js/data.js` - districts, armor, buildings, robots, bosses, badges, question generator
- `js/gl.js` - the WebGL box renderer and matrix helpers
- `js/models.js` - 3D hero, armor, weapons, robots, citizens, props
- `js/world.js` - the city (layout, collision, camera, HUD, minimap)
- `js/combat.js` - hero moves, weapons, robots, bosses, missions
- `js/vehicles.js` - car, helicopter, jet, parachute, robot bases, Robo-Pals
- `js/panels.js` - walk-in buildings, weapon shop, pause menu
- `js/art.js` - 2D drawing for the title screen and Dojo
- `js/dojo.js` - the three training mini-games
- `js/ui.js` - title, story, results, touch controls, main loop
- `node tools/serve.js` - optional local test server on http://localhost:8377
